Advisories

Valid raw GTINs open product information automatically. Eight-digit codes require EAN-8 or UPC-E decoder metadata, or an explicit choice on the interpretation page.

Point the camera at a Safebox record-sharing or presentation code, Clear token, NUT-18 Clear payment request, Lightning address or invoice, or GS1 Digital Link, or HTTPS website address. Scanning only acquires the request; Safebox determines the capability from the code before showing the next action.

The camera starts automatically. If access is unavailable, check the browser's camera permission or enter the code manually. Camera access from another device requires Safebox to be served over HTTPS.

Once text is acquired, the camera stops. Recognized GS1 Digital Links open Safebox’s independent product verification page, without visiting the printed website. Other HTTPS websites require a warning and confirmation before opening. Lightning/NIP-05 addresses, valid LNURLs encoding supported Lightning-address endpoints, and Clear payment requests open payment review automatically; payment still requires confirmation. Other codes go to a separate interpretation page. EAN, UPC and DataMatrix camera decoding depends on browser support. Preserve ASCII group separators when pasting GS1 element strings.
