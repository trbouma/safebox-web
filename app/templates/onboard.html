Safebox lets you create a new Acorn or reconnect one you already control. An Acorn keeps its keys, funds, and records portable across applications and infrastructure.

Use an Existing Acorn

One-click creation temporarily keeps the Safebox Acorn mnemonic in this browser's encrypted session instead of displaying it immediately. The new wallet opens at once, but the recovery backup must be completed before the session expires or is cleared. Safebox also assigns a default public handle derived from the Acorn public key; it can be changed or removed later. Protected records can be enabled later.
