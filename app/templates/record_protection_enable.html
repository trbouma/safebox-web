Protected Records are optional. Enabling them creates an independent Record Protection Key and a separately labelled 24-word mnemonic. Neither the key nor its mnemonic is stored on a relay.

Records created before activation remain ordinary Acorn records. A future explicit migration will be required to protect an existing record.

Bring your own record-protection entropy

Optionally supply exactly 32 bytes of independently generated entropy as 64 hexadecimal characters. Do not reuse wallet entropy, a password, or other guessable material.
