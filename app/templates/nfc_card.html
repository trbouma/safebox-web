Test an NFC card

Use a writable NDEF card and a compatible NFC-enabled device/browser over HTTPS. Browser API availability alone does not confirm working hardware.

This writes exactly one public text record:

SAFEBOX-WEB-NFC-TEST:v1

Writing replaces existing NDEF content. This does not permanently lock the card or change its server-side activation or revocation status. Locked cards cannot be overwritten.

Writing and reading are separate actions. After writing, remove the card, choose Read Any NFC Card, then tap it again. You can also read an existing safebox-2 or other NDEF card without changing it. The reader shows record types and byte counts, but hides potentially sensitive payloads and card identifiers. It does not follow links, submit contents or log you in. Reading successfully does not confirm write permission or a card's resistance to copying.
