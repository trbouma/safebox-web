Cash Transfer Mode
Advisories

The transfer amount and the mint's fee reserve must fit within one keyset accepted by the mint. The displayed total balance may therefore be greater than the amount available for one transfer.

Confirmed mode checks the mint and can use Lightning when the address is outside Safebox.

Continuity mode does not contact the mint or use Lightning. It works only with another Safebox address and only when the wallet already holds an exact set of proofs for the amount. The recipient holds those proofs provisionally until mint reconciliation is available.

A Clear transfer spends from exactly one issuing mint and canonical CMU. It is sent privately to a compatible Safebox address and never mixed with the Cash Balance. Cash Transfer Mode does not apply to Clear transfers.

Scanning is available from the wallet home page.
